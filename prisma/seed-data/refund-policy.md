# FlowPilot Demo Co. — Refund Policy

Version 4.2 · Owner: Finance Operations · Last reviewed: 12 March

## 1. Scope

This policy governs all customer refunds issued through FlowPilot, whether initiated by a
support operator, by the AI operations agent, or automatically by the payments platform.
It applies to every order placed on the store, marketplace and phone channels.

## 2. Standard refund window

Customers can request a refund within 30 days of delivery. The 30-day clock starts on the
date the carrier marks the order as delivered, not on the order date. Requests received
after 30 days are declined by default and require a manager override to proceed.

Orders that have not yet shipped can be cancelled and refunded at any time before dispatch,
with no eligibility questions asked. Cancellation before dispatch always results in a full
refund, including any delivery charges collected.

## 3. Eligibility by order state

- **Pending or processing** — full refund, immediate.
- **Shipped but not delivered** — full refund once the parcel is returned to the warehouse,
  or immediately if the carrier confirms the parcel is lost.
- **Delivered** — refundable within the 30-day window provided the item is unused and in its
  original packaging. Opened consumables and personalised items are not refundable.
- **Cancelled** — no refund is due because no payment was captured.
- **Already refunded** — no further refund is possible; partial refunds may top up to the
  original order value but never beyond it.

## 4. Delivery delays

When an order misses its promised delivery date by more than 5 working days, the customer is
entitled to a full refund on request even if the parcel is still in transit. Operators do not
need a manager approval for delay-related refunds under ₹25,000.

Goodwill credit of up to ₹500 may be offered in addition to a delay refund. Anything larger
must be approved by a team lead.

## 5. Partial refunds

Partial refunds are permitted for multi-item orders where only some items are returned, and
for service-level failures where the customer keeps the goods. The refunded amount can never
exceed the amount actually captured, minus any refunds already issued against that order.

## 6. Approval thresholds

- Refunds up to ₹25,000 — an operator with the refund permission may approve.
- Refunds above ₹25,000 — require a second approver from the finance team.
- Refunds on orders older than 30 days — require a manager override with a written reason.

Every refund, regardless of amount, is recorded in the audit log with the requesting user,
the approver, the amount and the reason.

## 7. Processing time

Refunds are submitted to the payment provider immediately on approval. Card refunds settle
in 5–7 working days; UPI and wallet refunds usually settle within 48 hours. Customers should
be told the settlement window, not a specific date.

## 8. What operators must never do

- Never promise a refund before checking the order's payment status.
- Never issue a refund against an unpaid or failed payment.
- Never issue a duplicate refund without checking the existing refund history for the order.
- Never quote refund eligibility from memory — always check this policy.
