# Warranty and Returns Handbook

Version 3.1 · Owner: Product Support · Last reviewed: 20 January

## 1. Standard warranty

All electronics carry a 12-month manufacturer warranty from the delivery date. Accessories
carry 6 months. Consumables, batteries and software licences carry no warranty.

The warranty covers manufacturing defects and component failure under normal use. It does not
cover physical damage, liquid ingress, unauthorised repair, or wear and tear.

## 2. Extended warranty

Customers may purchase an extended warranty within 30 days of delivery, adding either 12 or
24 months. Extended warranty claims follow the same process as standard claims but are routed
to the extended-cover queue.

## 3. Return window

Unopened items may be returned within 15 days of delivery for a full refund. Opened items may
be returned within 15 days only if faulty. Personalised or made-to-order items cannot be
returned unless faulty.

Return shipping is free when the item is faulty or was sent in error. Otherwise the customer
pays a flat ₹199 return fee, deducted from the refund.

## 4. Raising a warranty claim

1. Confirm the delivery date and that the item is inside its warranty period.
2. Collect a description of the fault and, where possible, photographs.
3. Create a `PRODUCT` category ticket at `HIGH` priority for hardware failures, `MEDIUM`
   otherwise.
4. Arrange a pickup; the service centre has 7 working days to assess.

## 5. Outcomes

- **Repair** — default outcome for a first hardware failure inside warranty.
- **Replacement** — when repair is not economical or the same fault recurs twice.
- **Refund** — when a replacement is unavailable, or on the third failure of the same unit.

## 6. Dead on arrival

An item that fails within 48 hours of delivery is treated as dead on arrival. DOA units are
replaced immediately without a service-centre assessment, and the customer keeps their
delivery slot priority.

## 7. Out-of-warranty repairs

Out-of-warranty repairs are chargeable. Quote the assessment fee up front, and never commit to
a repair price before the service centre has inspected the unit.
